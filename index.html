THE STRATEGY

先做对的研究，
再做漂亮的模型。

教授明确强调技术深度、严格实验、批判性分析与项目执行。项目应当解释“为什么有效”，而不是只汇报一个准确率。The course prioritizes technical depth, experimental rigor, insight, and execution. Explain why the model works, not just its final score.

Scale-aware feature fusion for aerial small-object detection
